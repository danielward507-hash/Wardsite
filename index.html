About Ward Detail Co.

One pair of hands.
One standard.

Ward Detail Co. is owner-operated — every car is washed, valeted and detailed by the person whose name is on the business. No rushed jobs, no rota of staff, no cutting corners to squeeze more cars in.

That means safe wash methods that protect your paint, products chosen for your car rather than whatever's cheapest, and a finish that's checked panel by panel before we call it done.

  • Safe two-bucket wash method — no swirl-inducing shortcuts
  • Professional products on every job, not forecourt gear
  • Flexible slots, including evenings and weekends
  • Regular-clean plans for weekly or monthly upkeep
